# Premier League All-Time XI Builder

Build your dream Premier League all-time XI with a visual pitch layout and flexible formations. Choose from 10 different formations including 4-4-2, 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, and more. Enter custom player names for each position, use the randomizer to generate a team from Premier League legends, and share your lineup with a single click.
